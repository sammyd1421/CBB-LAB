What this first version knows

HISTORICAL PATTERN FINDER

Daily Slate Scanner

Scan every scheduled game on a date against prior-season matchup fingerprints.

Default balance: average similarity 40%, total volatility 30%, scoring-margin volatility 30%. Expand any game for its historical breakdown and 80%+ twins. Home and away teams plus venue are taken directly from the schedule. Today's spread, total and sportsbook prices are never used. Similarity grades: 95–100 nearly identical, 90–94 elite, 80–89 strong, 70–79 above average, 60–69 moderate; below 60 is weak or poor and excluded.
